PIXEL ICONS
===========

Named after the mod, addressed by convention as icons/<mod id>.gif (a .png
of the same name works too - the page tries gif, then png, then takes the
slot away). Nothing is declared anywhere: drop a file in and it appears,
take it away and it vanishes and the layout closes up.

WHAT WENT WHERE
---------------
    archive.gif         the tower on fire - the header, not a mod
    fumes.gif           a tank of orange liquid
    bare-minimum.gif    a battery reading FULL
    five0patrol.gif     a police light, turning
    hoodrich.gif        a bag of product
    weapon-tweaks.gif   a pistol
    vehicle-tweaks.gif  switches and breakers
    bloodymess.gif      blood, pooling
    overspray.gif       a tower with its guts spilled out
    franklin-rp.gif     the man in the green bandana
    streetgolf.gif      a wreck

Some of those are judgement calls rather than obvious matches. Swap any
two files and the site follows - there is nothing else to change.

SPARES
------
    spare/transmitter.gif      spare/green-terminal.gif
    spare/3d-printer.gif

Move one up a level under a mod's name to put it into rotation.

EACH ONE APPEARS FOUR TIMES
---------------------------
    42px   index row, left column
    76px   head of its dossier, middle column
    20px   beside ID//BUILD in the spec panel, right column
    92px   archive.gif only, top right of the header

They are animated and they animate in place. Drawn with
image-rendering: pixelated, so keep them at native size - 64x64 here.

At rest they sit pulled most of the way down towards the page's amber and
come up to full colour on the row under the cursor, the row you have
selected, and the head of the open dossier. The colour is the selection.
